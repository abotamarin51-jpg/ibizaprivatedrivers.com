A direct arrival in Ibiza Town

Ibiza Town, also known as Eivissa, includes the historic centre, the port, Figueretes and nearby waterfront areas. The exact address matters because pedestrian streets and access controls can change the closest safe drop-off point.

For Dalt Vila accommodation, share the hotel name and a map pin before arrival. Your driver can confirm the practical vehicle access point rather than leaving the decision until you reach the old town.

Every booking is planned around the confirmed pickup time, passenger count, luggage and final destination. Share a flight number for airport arrivals so the collection can be coordinated with the scheduled landing information.

The service is private: you do not share the vehicle with unrelated passengers. Child seats, extra luggage, several vehicles or additional stops must be requested before confirmation so availability can be checked.

Journey times vary with traffic, road works, access restrictions and the exact address. We confirm the operational plan rather than promising an unrealistic fixed travel time.

What information is required to book?

Send the date, pickup time, passenger number, luggage, pickup point and exact destination. For an airport arrival, include the flight number.

Can I request a return journey or several vehicles?

Yes. Add the return time, group size and any separate arrival details so the required vehicles and timings can be checked before confirmation.
