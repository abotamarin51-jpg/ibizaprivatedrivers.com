FAQ

Private taxi questions

Is there a private taxi service in Ibiza?
Yes. We are a private taxi and private driver service in Ibiza. Instead of waiting at a rank or hailing a cab, you pre-book a Mercedes V-Class with a professional driver at a fixed, all-inclusive price.
What is the difference between a private taxi and a normal Ibiza taxi?
A normal Ibiza taxi is hailed or taken from a rank with a meter. A private taxi with us is pre-booked, with a fixed price agreed in advance, a guaranteed luxury vehicle, meet and greet and a professional driver — no meter and no waiting in line.
How much does a private taxi in Ibiza cost?
Prices are fixed and depend on the route, starting from around 100 euros. You get the full price up front with no meter and no surprises. Message us with your journey for an exact quote.
Can I book a private taxi in Ibiza in advance?
Yes, and we recommend it, especially in peak season. Pre-booking guarantees your vehicle and driver, a fixed price and meet and greet. We operate 24/7 and confirm quickly via WhatsApp.
Do you offer private taxi transfers from Ibiza Airport?
Yes. Airport transfers are our core service. Your private driver tracks your flight, meets you in arrivals with a name board and takes you straight to your destination across the island.
