Private driver for several days

One transport plan across the stay

A multi-day chauffeur booking is designed for travellers who need more than individual point-to-point transfers. Each day can have its own confirmed schedule while the overall transport plan stays coordinated through one service.

The booking does not imply unlimited hours or automatic 24-hour availability. Daily start times, expected duration, known stops and late-night or early-morning requirements should be provided so availability and pricing can be reviewed accurately.

Frequently asked questions

Multi-day chauffeur service in Ibiza

Can I book a private chauffeur in Ibiza for several days?

Yes. Multi-day chauffeur bookings can be requested for consecutive or selected days, subject to confirmed driver and vehicle availability. Send the dates, expected daily hours and known itinerary.

Can the itinerary change from day to day?

Yes. Each day can have a different confirmed plan. Changes remain subject to timing, access, driver and vehicle availability, and any revised quote or service conditions.

Can the service cover villas, hotels, yachts and nightlife?

Yes. Confirmed road itineraries can include hotels, private villas, marinas, yachts, restaurants, beaches, meetings, events and nightlife across Ibiza.

Can several vehicles be arranged?

Additional vehicles can be requested for families, assistants, guest groups or separate schedules, subject to passenger count, luggage and availability.

Is the chauffeur automatically available 24 hours a day throughout the stay?

No. The requested daily start times, duration, itinerary and any late-night or early-morning requirements must be included and confirmed in the booking.
