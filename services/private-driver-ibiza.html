Transfer or chauffeur service?

A point-to-point transfer covers one confirmed pickup and destination. It works for an airport arrival, hotel departure, villa journey or marina connection when the date, time and route are known in advance.

A chauffeur booking is better when the vehicle is needed for several stops or for a longer period. Send the full itinerary, including restaurants, beaches, meetings, marinas, events and the final return. If the plan is flexible, explain what is known and which decisions may be made during the service.

A later collection is not automatically included with a one-way transfer. Add every return time before confirmation. If passengers leave an event at different times, request separate journeys or vehicles rather than assuming one booking covers the whole group.

The service can be requested 24 hours a day, but availability is not automatic. The date, route, vehicle arrangement and booking terms must be confirmed for the specific request.

Information to send

A complete request receives a useful answer

Start with the date, requested pickup time and exact pickup and destination. Add the passenger count, luggage, child-seat requirements, intermediate stops and return. For a rural villa, send a map pin and access notes. For a marina, send the marina, yacht or berth details. For an event, use the practical meeting point stated in the confirmation.

For Ibiza Airport, include the flight number instead of only an estimated message after landing. If guests arrive on different flights, explain whether they should be collected separately or wait for one departure.

Large suitcases, sports equipment and several passengers can change the suitable vehicle arrangement. The number of seats alone does not confirm luggage capacity. Send the actual luggage information before booking.

If the schedule changes, request an updated confirmation. A different pickup time, route, passenger group or number of stops can affect availability and the quote.

Frequently asked questions

Before booking a private driver

What private-driver service should I book in Ibiza?

Choose a point-to-point transfer for one confirmed journey, airport transfer for an IBZ arrival or departure, or chauffeur service for several stops and a longer itinerary.

What information is required for a quote?

Send the date, pickup time, exact pickup and destination, passengers, luggage, child-seat requests, stops and return. Include the flight number for an airport arrival.

Can I book several journeys or vehicles?

Yes. Send every route, passenger group and requested time so availability, vehicle requirements and coordination can be reviewed.

Is a return automatically included?

No. A return or later collection must be listed and confirmed separately unless it appears in the written booking.
