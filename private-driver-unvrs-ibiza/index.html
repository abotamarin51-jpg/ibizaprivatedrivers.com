Planning the San Rafael Pickup

Event traffic and access conditions can change during busy nights. Journey time should therefore be treated as variable rather than guaranteed. Traffic, road works, temporary controls and the exact pickup point can all affect the operational plan.

For the return journey, keep your phone available and follow the meeting instructions in the confirmed booking. If a preferred pickup position cannot be used at that moment, an alternative permitted meeting location can be agreed.

Transport is separate from the event itself. A driver reservation does not include admission, event tickets, VIP tables or venue hospitality unless another service has been expressly arranged and confirmed.
