Local business identity

A private chauffeur company built around precise Ibiza coordination

Ibiza Private Drivers is operated by IBIZA PRIVATE DRIVERS S.L. and based in Ibiza, Balearic Islands, Spain. Founder Juan Cruz leads the company’s local coordination for pre-arranged private transport.

The service covers Ibiza Airport, luxury hotels, private villas, ports and marinas, restaurants, beaches, nightlife, business meetings, events and tailored multi-stop itineraries. Every request is reviewed using the actual date, local pickup time, route or itinerary, passengers, luggage, vehicle requirements and expected duration.

Ibiza is the road-service area. Countries mentioned across the website identify client-origin and B2B acquisition markets; they are not presented as additional operating locations.
