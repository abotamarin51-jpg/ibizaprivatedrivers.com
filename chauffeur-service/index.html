FAQ

Chauffeur hire questions

How much does it cost to hire a chauffeur in Ibiza?

Hourly, half-day, full-day and itinerary-based chauffeur bookings can be requested. Pricing is quoted for the confirmed schedule, passenger count and vehicle requirements.

Can I hire a driver by the hour rather than the full day?

Yes. We offer both. Hourly hire suits an evening out, a dinner or a short business engagement; full-day suits island touring, weddings and corporate itineraries. Contact us for a personalised rate.

What occasions is the chauffeur service used for?

Weddings, corporate trips, VIP guests, nights out, restaurant-hopping, beach days and full-island touring. Wherever you need to be, your driver is on hand for the day or evening.

What vehicle is used?

A Mercedes-Benz V-Class can be requested. Passenger, luggage and vehicle requirements are confirmed before booking.

Are your drivers discreet and professional?

Yes. Professional, experienced with private bookings, and focused on discretion. Routes and meeting details are planned for the confirmed itinerary.

Do you operate at night and for late events?

Yes, 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Late-night events, club pick-ups and early-morning departures are fully covered subject to confirmed availability and booking terms.
