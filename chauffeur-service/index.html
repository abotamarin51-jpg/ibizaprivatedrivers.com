FAQ

Chauffeur hire questions

How much does it cost to hire a chauffeur in Ibiza?

Full-day chauffeur hire starts from €600 in a Mercedes V-Class, and hourly hire is available for shorter needs. All rates are fixed and all-inclusive — fuel, waiting time and the driver are covered, with no meter running.

Can I hire a driver by the hour rather than the full day?

Yes. We offer both. Hourly hire suits an evening out, a dinner or a short business engagement; full-day suits island touring, weddings and corporate itineraries. Contact us for a personalised rate.

What occasions is the chauffeur service used for?

Weddings, corporate trips, VIP guests, nights out, restaurant-hopping, beach days and full-island touring. Wherever you need to be, your driver is on hand for the day or evening.

What vehicle is used?

A new Mercedes V-Class — air-conditioned, spacious and discreet, seating up to seven with generous luggage space, complimentary water and WiFi on board.

Are your drivers discreet and professional?

Yes. Fully licensed, experienced with high-profile and private clientele, with confidentiality guaranteed. They know every road, venue and marina on the island.

Do you operate at night and for late events?

Yes, 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Late-night events, club pick-ups and early-morning departures are fully covered with no unsocial-hours surcharge.
