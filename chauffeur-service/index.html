Transfer or chauffeur?

When a chauffeur booking makes more sense

A point-to-point transfer is designed for one confirmed pickup and destination. A chauffeur booking is more suitable when the schedule includes several movements, waiting time or a vehicle that remains connected to the itinerary for an agreed period.

The requested duration does not automatically define unlimited travel or unlisted stops. Send the full schedule—or as much as is known—so the driver, vehicle, timing and quote can be confirmed for the actual plan. For a single journey, see our Ibiza Transfers page.

Booking details

What to send for an accurate chauffeur quote

1. Date and start time

Include the requested service date and first collection time.

2. Pickup and itinerary

List the exact starting point and every known hotel, villa, restaurant, beach, marina, event or meeting.

3. Duration and return

Give the estimated duration and final return destination rather than assuming the vehicle remains available indefinitely.

4. Passengers and luggage

Include group size, luggage, child-seat requirements and whether several vehicles may be needed.

Pricing is tailored to the confirmed schedule, requested duration, stops, waiting, passenger count and vehicle needs. No generic hourly or daily figure is assumed to apply to every itinerary.
