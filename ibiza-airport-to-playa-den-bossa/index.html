FAQ

Ibiza Airport → Playa d'en Bossa questions

How much is a transfer from Ibiza Airport to Playa d'en Bossa?
A private transfer starts from €100 in a Mercedes V-Class. The price is fixed and all-inclusive — luggage, child seats and waiting time are covered, with no surcharge for night arrivals.
How long does the journey take?
Under ten minutes. Playa d'en Bossa is roughly 4 km from the airport, the closest resort area on the island.
Will the driver meet me inside the airport?
Yes. Your driver waits in the arrivals hall with a name board. We track your flight in real time, so even if you are delayed there is no extra charge.
Can you drop me at a specific hotel or club?
Always. This is a private door-to-door service to any hotel, apartment or club in Playa d'en Bossa, including Ushuaia, Hi and the Hard Rock Hotel.
Do you operate late at night?
Yes, 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Late-night and early-morning arrivals are fully covered with no unsocial-hours surcharge.
