Can the driver collect me directly outside a club or event?

The closest permitted pickup point is confirmed according to access conditions. Large events may require a nearby meeting location.

Can I travel with several suitcases or sports equipment?

Yes, if declared before booking. Send the number and approximate size of the items so the appropriate vehicle can be checked.

What information is required to book?

Send the date, pickup time, passenger number, luggage, pickup point and exact destination. For an airport arrival, include the flight number.

Can I request a return journey or several vehicles?

Yes. Add the return time, group size and any separate arrival details so the required vehicles and timings can be checked before confirmation.
