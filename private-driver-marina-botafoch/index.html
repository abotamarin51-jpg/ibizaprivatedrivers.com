Local planning · Private service

Precise pickup details matter at the marina

Marina Botafoch can refer to the wider neighbourhood, a specific marina entrance, a yacht berth or a nearby hotel and restaurant area. Send the exact establishment, berth information or map pin so the vehicle is directed to the correct side of the waterfront.

For yacht arrivals and departures, include the yacht name when appropriate, the marina contact or meeting instruction, passenger count and luggage. Vehicle access to restricted quay areas cannot be assumed, so the final pedestrian meeting point should be confirmed with the captain, crew or marina representative.

Airport transfers should include every flight number if guests arrive separately. Explain whether the group should be collected independently or wait for a coordinated departure. Large luggage loads, sports equipment and child seats must be requested before confirmation.

Marina schedules can change with sailing plans, weather and guest movements. Keep the lead passenger reachable and notify the booking contact as early as possible if the pickup time or location changes. Availability for last-minute schedule changes cannot be guaranteed.

The service can also connect Marina Botafoch with Ibiza Town, Talamanca, private villas, restaurants and other ports. A multi-stop itinerary must be shared in full so it can be distinguished from a direct point-to-point transfer.

Frequently asked questions

Details to confirm before booking

What information is needed for a yacht pickup?

Send the marina, yacht or berth information, pickup time, passenger count, luggage and the contact person coordinating the meeting.

Can the vehicle drive directly to the yacht?

Access depends on marina rules and the specific berth. A practical meeting point is confirmed for the booking; restricted quay access is not guaranteed.

Can you collect guests arriving on different flights?

Yes, separate or coordinated pickups can be requested. Provide every flight number and explain the preferred group plan.

Do you also cover Talamanca and Ibiza Town?

Yes. Direct transfers and tailored itineraries can include nearby areas and confirmed destinations across Ibiza, subject to availability.
