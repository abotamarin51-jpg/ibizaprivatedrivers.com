How to Book a Hotel Transfer

1. Send the hotel and date

Include the exact property and requested journey date.

2. Add pickup details

For airport arrivals, include the flight number. For villas or marinas, send the exact meeting point.

3. Confirm passengers and luggage

Group size, luggage and child-seat needs affect vehicle planning.

4. Add return or itinerary

Include departure, restaurant, beach, marina or nightlife stops if you want the journey coordinated as one plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I book a private driver from Ibiza Airport to my hotel?

Yes. Send the flight number, scheduled arrival time, hotel name, passenger count, luggage and any child-seat or vehicle requirements so availability and the pickup plan can be confirmed.

Can I keep the same driver for restaurants and nightlife after hotel check-in?

A chauffeur booking can be requested for several hours or a tailored itinerary, subject to confirmed driver and vehicle availability.

Do you provide hotel departure transfers to Ibiza Airport?

Yes. Provide the hotel, flight departure details, passenger count and luggage. The collection time is agreed before confirmation rather than assumed from a generic journey time.

Are you affiliated with the hotels listed on this page?

No affiliation or endorsement is implied. Ibiza Private Drivers is an independent ground-transport provider offering pre-booked journeys to and from hotels and resorts in Ibiza.
