Airport, Hotel & Nightlife Planning

For airport arrivals, send the flight number and exact final destination. For hotel collections, use the correct hotel entrance or concierge meeting point. For nightlife, confirm both the outbound and return plan when possible.

Traffic and temporary access restrictions can change by date and hour, particularly during busy summer periods. Journey times should therefore be treated as estimates rather than guaranteed fixed durations.

If passengers are leaving at different times, request separate journeys or vehicles so the transport plan matches the group correctly.
