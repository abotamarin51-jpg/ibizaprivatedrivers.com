The Route

The quickest way from IBZ to the old town

Ibiza Town — Eivissa to the locals — sits just seven kilometres north-east of Ibiza Airport, making it one of the shortest transfers on the island, typically ten to fifteen minutes by road. Whether you are heading to a hotel by the marina, an apartment in Figueretas or the cobbled lanes of Dalt Vila, your private driver takes you straight to the door with no stops and no meter.

We track your flight from the moment it departs, so your Mercedes is ready whether you land early, on time or hours late. Your driver greets you inside the arrivals hall with a name board, takes care of the luggage, and you are on the road within minutes of stepping off the plane.

The Journey

Comfort from kerb to door

The road from the airport skirts the salt flats of Ses Salines before sweeping into Eivissa, with the floodlit walls of Dalt Vila rising ahead — a fitting first glimpse of the island for arriving guests.

For couples and small groups arriving with luggage, the V-Class offers room to stretch out after the flight. For late-night arrivals after a long travel day, it is simply the calmest way to reach your bed.

FAQ

Ibiza Airport → Ibiza Town questions

How much is a transfer from Ibiza Airport to Ibiza Town?
A private transfer from Ibiza Airport (IBZ) to Ibiza Town starts from €100 in a Mercedes V-Class. The price is fixed and all-inclusive — luggage, child seats and waiting time are covered, with no surcharge for night arrivals.
How long does the drive take?
Around ten to fifteen minutes. Ibiza Town is roughly 7 km from the airport, one of the shortest transfers on the island, though the exact time depends on traffic and your final address.
Will the driver meet me inside the airport?
Yes. Your driver waits in the arrivals hall with a name board. We track your flight in real time, so even if you are delayed there is no extra charge and no need to call ahead.
Can you drop me at a specific hotel or villa?
Always. This is a private door-to-door service — we take you to any hotel, apartment, villa or address in Ibiza Town, including the narrow streets of Dalt Vila and the marina.
Do you operate late at night?
Yes, 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Late-night and early-morning arrivals are fully covered with no unsocial-hours surcharge.
