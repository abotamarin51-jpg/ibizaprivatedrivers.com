From confirmed aviation pickup point to the rest of your Ibiza itinerary

Private aviation clients often need more than a single airport transfer. The onward plan may include a luxury hotel, private villa, yacht, marina, restaurant, meeting, security team, assistant or several passenger groups moving at different times.

The chauffeur service is planned around the confirmed pickup point and the real road itinerary. Restricted, airside or direct aircraft access is never assumed. Any special terminal or restricted-access arrangement must be separately confirmed with the relevant aviation operator or facility.

Booking information

What to send before confirmation

1. Aviation schedule

Arrival or departure date and time, plus any reference your coordinator wants included.

2. Passengers & luggage

Passenger count, luggage volume, child-seat needs and whether additional vehicles may be required.

3. Confirmed pickup point

The practical terminal or meeting point supplied by the aviation operator, handler or lead passenger.

4. Onward itinerary

Hotel, villa, marina, yacht, restaurant, meeting, event, expected duration and final return.

Access note: Ibiza Private Drivers does not represent or imply automatic FBO, terminal-restricted or airside access. The chauffeur follows the pickup point and access conditions confirmed for the specific booking.

Frequently asked questions

Private aviation chauffeur service in Ibiza

Can I pre-book a chauffeur for a private aviation arrival in Ibiza?

Yes. Send the arrival date and time, passenger count, luggage, contact details and onward destination. Pickup arrangements depend on the confirmed aviation terminal or practical meeting point and any access restrictions.

Can the chauffeur continue to a villa, luxury hotel or yacht?

Yes. The confirmed road itinerary can include hotels, villas, marinas, restaurants, meetings and other destinations across Ibiza. Exact villa pins and practical marina meeting points should be supplied before confirmation.

Can I keep the chauffeur for several hours after arrival?

Yes. Hourly, half-day, full-day and tailored chauffeur bookings can be requested, subject to confirmed availability. Send the estimated duration and all known stops.

Is airside or direct aircraft access included?

No access is assumed. Any restricted, airside or terminal-specific access must be separately confirmed with the relevant aviation operator or facility.

Can several vehicles be arranged?

Additional vehicles can be requested according to passenger count, luggage and the confirmed itinerary, subject to availability.
