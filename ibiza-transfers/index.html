Frequently asked questions

Booking private transfers in Ibiza

Is a private car service in Ibiza the same as a transfer?

For one confirmed pickup and destination, an Ibiza private car service can be booked as a point-to-point transfer. For waiting time, several stops or a retained vehicle, request chauffeur service instead.

Which Ibiza transfer should I book?

Choose airport transfer for IBZ arrivals or departures, point-to-point transfer for one confirmed pickup and destination, or chauffeur service for waiting time, several stops or a retained vehicle.

Can I book transfers to hotels, villas, marinas and clubs?

Yes. Confirmed journeys can be requested across Ibiza, subject to access, schedule, passengers, luggage and availability.

What information is needed for a transfer quote?

Send the date, pickup, destination, time, passengers, luggage and any child-seat, accessibility or vehicle requirements.

Can I arrange several Ibiza transfers together?

Yes. Send the complete schedule so separate journeys, timing and vehicle requirements can be reviewed under one request.
