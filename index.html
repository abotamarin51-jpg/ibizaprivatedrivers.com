Choose the right service

Transfer, chauffeur or multi-day private driver?

A private transfer is best for one confirmed pickup and destination, such as Ibiza Airport to a hotel, villa, marina or restaurant.

A chauffeur booking is more suitable when the day includes several movements, waiting time or a vehicle connected to an agreed itinerary for several hours.

For longer stays, a multi-day chauffeur can coordinate selected or consecutive days using the schedule, daily hours, passenger groups and vehicle requirements provided in advance.

Frequently asked questions

Booking a private driver in Ibiza

What is the difference between a transfer and a chauffeur booking?

A transfer covers a confirmed point-to-point journey. Chauffeur service is more suitable for several stops, waiting time or a vehicle connected to an itinerary for an agreed period.

Can I book a private driver for several days in Ibiza?

Yes. Multi-day chauffeur service can be requested for consecutive or selected days. Send each day’s start time, expected hours, known itinerary, passenger count and vehicle requirements.

Do you provide transport for private aviation passengers?

Yes. Send the flight or aviation details available to you, the confirmed practical collection point, passengers, luggage, onward destination and any requested chauffeur itinerary. Restricted or airside access is never assumed.

Can a travel advisor or luxury concierge book for a client?

Yes. Travel advisors, luxury concierges, villa managers, yacht professionals, personal assistants and hospitality teams can request guest transport and multi-vehicle itinerary coordination.

Can service be requested late at night or early in the morning?

Yes. Bookings can be requested for any hour, including early-morning and late-night schedules, subject to advance reservation and confirmed driver and vehicle availability.

Do you operate road transport in Formentera?

The core road service is in Ibiza. Formentera-related requests generally involve private transport in Ibiza to Ibiza Port for an onward ferry connection. Any separate service in Formentera must be specifically confirmed.
