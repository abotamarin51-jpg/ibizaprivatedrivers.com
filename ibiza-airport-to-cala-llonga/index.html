Private transfer to Cala Llonga & Es Canar

Cala Llonga and Es Canar sit on Ibiza's gentle east coast, around twenty to twenty-four kilometres from the airport - a relaxed twenty-five to thirty-minute drive. These calm, family-friendly resorts near Santa Eulalia are among the island's most popular bases for a quieter holiday.

From a beachfront hotel in Es Canar to a villa above Cala Llonga, your private driver takes you straight to the door - no stops, no meter. We track your flight, meet you inside arrivals, and handle the luggage.
