A planned arrival for Ushuaïa Ibiza

Ushuaïa Ibiza is an open-air venue and hotel complex in Playa d’en Bossa. Event traffic, pedestrian areas and temporary access instructions can affect where a vehicle can stop, so every booking is planned around the confirmed date, entrance and meeting point rather than a generic venue name.

If you are travelling directly from Ibiza Airport, send the flight number, passenger count and luggage before confirmation. Guests arriving with suitcases may need a different vehicle plan from passengers travelling from a nearby hotel or villa for an event.

For the return journey, provide the preferred collection time and a reachable mobile number. Event exits can be busy, and the safest practical pickup point may be a short walk from the main entrance. The exact plan is confirmed for the booking instead of promising an unrealistic door position.

Groups arriving on different flights or staying at different properties can request coordinated pickups. Include every hotel, villa or map pin and explain whether the group must arrive together. Additional vehicles and stops are subject to availability and must be agreed in advance.

Ibiza Private Drivers is an independent transport provider and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Ushuaïa Ibiza. Event admission, opening times and venue policies must be checked directly with the official venue.

Frequently asked questions

Details to confirm before booking

Can I book directly from Ibiza Airport to Ushuaïa?

Yes. Send the flight number, landing time, passenger count, luggage and whether the destination is the venue or hotel reception.

Can the driver collect us after the event?

Yes, subject to availability. Agree the collection time, practical meeting point and final destination before the booking is confirmed.

Can several hotel pickups be combined?

They can be requested. Send every pickup address and guest count so the required time and vehicle plan can be reviewed.

Is admission to Ushuaïa included?

No. Ibiza Private Drivers provides transport only. Tickets, guest lists, table bookings and venue policies are managed separately by the venue or booking provider.
