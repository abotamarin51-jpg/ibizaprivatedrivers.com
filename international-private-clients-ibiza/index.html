Market-specific planning

What international guests should organise before they fly

United States and United Kingdom. Send the final flight arriving into Ibiza, not only the original departure. Use local Ibiza time for every service. American travellers may search for an “Ibiza car service” while UK travellers may prefer “private driver” or “chauffeur”; a point-to-point car service is handled as a transfer, while waiting time and several stops require a chauffeur booking. Read the USA and UK Ibiza private-driver guide.

France, Monaco, Switzerland, Germany and Luxembourg. Provide the exact accommodation, complete itinerary and preferred communication language. French-speaking travellers can use our chauffeur privé Ibiza page; German-speaking travellers can use our Privatchauffeur Ibiza page. Switzerland is served through the appropriate French or German content, while every road journey remains in Ibiza.

United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Kuwait. For family groups, assistants or several vehicles, send one operational lead contact and the real passenger and luggage distribution. Share villa pins, hotel reservations, yacht meeting points and expected service hours before confirmation. Arabic-language planning is available on our private chauffeur Ibiza page.

Singapore and Hong Kong. Long-haul guests should send the final Ibiza arrival segment, local service dates, luggage and all fixed reservations. Travel advisors and concierge teams can use the Ibiza B2B chauffeur partner page to coordinate several client movements.

Frequently asked questions

International private chauffeur service in Ibiza

Do you provide chauffeur service outside Ibiza?

No additional service areas are claimed on this page. The countries listed are where clients and partners originate; the road service described is delivered in Ibiza.

Can a travel advisor or concierge book for a client?

Yes. Send the complete itinerary and the professional contact responsible for coordination and changes.

What should international clients send before arriving?

Send the date, local Ibiza pickup time, exact locations, flight details, passengers, luggage, return requirements and expected chauffeur duration.

Can airport, villa, yacht, hotel and nightlife transport be combined?

Yes. Several confirmed journeys or chauffeur periods can be reviewed under one itinerary, subject to availability, access conditions and agreed pricing.
