FAQ

Luxury transfer questions

What is a private transfer in Ibiza?
It is pre-booked private-driver transport for a confirmed route or itinerary. It is not a rank or street-hail taxi service.
What details are confirmed before travel?
The route, date, time, passengers, luggage, vehicle requirements, meeting plan and quote are reviewed before booking.
How much is a private transfer in Ibiza?
Pricing is quoted for the confirmed route, date, time, passenger count, luggage, stops and vehicle requirements.
Can I book a private driver for the whole day in Ibiza?
Hourly, half-day, full-day and itinerary-based service can be requested, subject to availability.
Do you cover Ibiza Airport transfers?
Yes. Send the flight number, scheduled landing time, passengers, luggage and exact destination so the pickup plan can be coordinated.
