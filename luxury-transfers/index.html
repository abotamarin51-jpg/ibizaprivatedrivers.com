What “luxury transfer” means on this site

A luxury transfer is a private journey arranged before travel with the requested pickup, destination, passengers, luggage and practical meeting details confirmed. It can be used for an airport arrival, hotel departure, villa collection, marina connection, restaurant reservation or event journey.

This page focuses on premium **transfer** intent. If you need a vehicle for several hours and multiple stops, use our Private Chauffeur Service Ibiza. For the broad island-wide service entity, see Private Driver Ibiza.

Frequently asked questions

Luxury transfers in Ibiza

What is a luxury private transfer in Ibiza?

It is pre-booked private-driver transport for a confirmed route or itinerary, with passenger, luggage, vehicle and meeting details reviewed before travel.

Can I book a luxury transfer from Ibiza Airport to a hotel or villa?

Yes. Send the flight number, scheduled arrival time, passengers, luggage and exact hotel or villa destination so the pickup plan can be confirmed.

How much does a luxury transfer in Ibiza cost?

Pricing depends on the confirmed route or itinerary, date, time, passenger count, luggage, stops, waiting requirements and vehicle needs. A tailored quote is provided before confirmation.

Can I book several luxury transfers during my stay?

Yes. Send the full schedule of airport, hotel, villa, marina, restaurant, event and return journeys so each movement and any required vehicles can be reviewed together.

Can I keep a chauffeur available instead of booking separate transfers?

Yes. For several stops or a longer period, an hourly, half-day, full-day or tailored chauffeur itinerary can be requested, subject to confirmed availability.
