Local planning · Private service

Night transport planned around your schedule

Hï Ibiza is located in Playa d’en Bossa. On event nights the surrounding roads, pedestrian movement and designated stopping areas can become busy, so the booking should include the event date, intended arrival time and a reachable contact number.

A single transfer is suitable when the pickup and destination are fixed. If you want a driver available across dinner, several venues or a late return to a villa, send the full itinerary so chauffeur availability can be checked for the complete time window.

For airport arrivals followed by a night event, luggage changes the plan. Tell us whether the group will stop at a hotel first or travel with luggage, and include the exact accommodation address. Never leave luggage requirements until the pickup time.

Return collections require a practical meeting point. Large crowds can make the entrance unsuitable for immediate vehicle access, and phone connectivity can be inconsistent. The driver and lead passenger should agree the meeting instruction before the group enters the venue.

Ibiza Private Drivers is an independent transport provider and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Hï Ibiza. Customers should confirm tickets, opening times, admission requirements and the official event schedule directly with the venue.

Frequently asked questions

Details to confirm before booking

Can I reserve only the journey to Hï Ibiza?

Yes. A one-way or return transfer can be requested. Send the pickup address, date, time and passenger number.

Can I keep a chauffeur available during the night?

Hourly or itinerary-based chauffeur service can be requested, subject to availability. Provide the full expected schedule for a tailored quote.

Where will the driver collect us after the event?

The meeting point is agreed for the specific booking. Access can vary, so follow the driver’s confirmed instructions rather than relying on a generic entrance name.

Does the transport booking include club tickets?

No. The service covers private transportation only. Admission and venue services must be arranged separately.
