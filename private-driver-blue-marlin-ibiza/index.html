Blue Marlin Ibiza is located at Cala Jondal in the Sant Josep area of Ibiza. For transport, the most useful information is the exact pickup address, requested arrival time, passenger count and whether you need a return or a chauffeur to remain connected to a wider itinerary.

Planning the Cala Jondal Pickup

Cala Jondal can become busy during popular service periods and events. Journey time should therefore be treated as variable rather than guaranteed. Traffic, road conditions, temporary access controls and the exact pickup point can all affect the operational plan.

For the return journey, keep your phone available and use the pickup instructions confirmed with the driver. If the preferred meeting point cannot be used at that moment, an alternative permitted location can be agreed.

Venue bookings are separate from transport. A driver reservation does not itself include beach beds, restaurant reservations, lounge or club tables, event admission or other Blue Marlin hospitality services.
