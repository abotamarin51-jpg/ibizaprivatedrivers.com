When a night chauffeur makes sense

A single nightlife transfer is designed for one confirmed pickup and destination. The night-driver service is designed for evenings with several movements, waiting or a chauffeur retained for an agreed period.

Send the date, first pickup time, passengers, pickup location, known destinations, estimated duration and final return. The requested hours do not automatically mean unlimited travel or unlisted stops; the actual itinerary is confirmed before booking.

For one point-to-point journey, use Ibiza Private Transfers. For a broader daytime or mixed itinerary, use Chauffeur Ibiza.

Frequently asked questions

Ibiza night driver booking

Can I book a private driver for several hours at night?

Yes. Send the date, pickup time, expected duration, passengers, known stops and final return so an evening chauffeur itinerary can be confirmed.

Can the chauffeur wait while we are at dinner or a nightclub?

Waiting and multi-stop schedules can be requested as part of an hourly or itinerary-based chauffeur booking, subject to the confirmed terms.

Can I book only one nightlife transfer instead?

Yes. For one confirmed pickup and destination, request a point-to-point private transfer.

Which nightlife areas can be covered?

Bookings can be requested across Ibiza, subject to confirmed availability and the specific itinerary.
