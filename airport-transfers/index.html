FAQ

Airport transfer questions

How much is a private transfer from Ibiza Airport?

Pricing is quoted for the confirmed route, date, time, passenger count, luggage and vehicle requirements. Waiting and additional-stop terms are stated before booking.

Will the driver meet me inside Ibiza Airport?

The airport meeting point, flight details and applicable waiting terms are confirmed with the booking. Contact us if the schedule changes.

Which destinations do you cover from the airport?

Confirmed destinations across Ibiza can be requested, including Ibiza Town, Santa Eulalia, San Antonio, Playa d'en Bossa, Es Canar, hotels, villas and the Ibiza Port connection for Formentera.

Do you operate late at night and early morning?

Bookings can be requested at any time of day, subject to confirmed availability and booking terms.

What vehicle will I travel in?

A Mercedes-Benz V-Class can be requested. Passenger, luggage and vehicle requirements are confirmed before booking.

How do I book an airport transfer?

Book via WhatsApp or email. We confirm availability, the agreed meeting details and the accepted payment method before the journey.
