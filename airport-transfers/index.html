Airport pricing

A quote based on the actual journey

There is no single airport price that accurately represents every Ibiza destination. Pricing depends on the confirmed route, date, requested pickup time, passengers, luggage, waiting or additional stops and vehicle requirements.

Formentera references on this site principally describe transport from Ibiza Airport to Ibiza Port for onward ferry connections. They should not be read as a claim that the core road-transfer service operates across Formentera.

Frequently asked questions

Ibiza Airport private transfers

How much is a private transfer from Ibiza Airport?

Pricing depends on the confirmed destination, date, pickup time, passengers, luggage, stops, waiting requirements and vehicle needs. A tailored quote is provided before booking.

What information should I send for an Ibiza Airport pickup?

Send the airline or flight number, scheduled arrival time, passenger count, luggage, exact destination and any child-seat or vehicle requirements.

Will the driver meet me at Ibiza Airport?

The practical airport meeting point, contact method, flight information and applicable waiting terms are confirmed with the booking.

Which destinations can I request from Ibiza Airport?

Confirmed destinations across Ibiza can be requested, including Ibiza Town, Playa d'en Bossa, Talamanca, Marina Botafoch, Santa Eulalia, San Antonio, hotels, villas and Ibiza Port for onward ferry connections.

Can I book a late-night or early-morning airport transfer?

Yes. Airport journeys can be requested for any time of day, subject to advance reservation and confirmed driver and vehicle availability.

Can I book a transfer from my hotel or villa back to Ibiza Airport?

Yes. Provide the departure flight information, exact pickup address, passengers and luggage so an appropriate collection time and transport plan can be agreed.
