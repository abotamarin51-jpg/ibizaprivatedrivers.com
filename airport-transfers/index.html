How it works

From arrivals hall to your door

FAQ

Airport transfer questions

How much is a private transfer from Ibiza Airport?

Private airport transfers start from €100 in a Mercedes V-Class, depending on your destination. All rates are fixed and all-inclusive — luggage, child seats and waiting time covered, with no surcharge for night arrivals.

Will the driver meet me inside Ibiza Airport?

Yes. Your driver waits in the arrivals hall with a name board and helps with your luggage. We track your flight in real time, so even if you are delayed there is no extra charge and no need to call ahead.

Which destinations do you cover from the airport?

Every destination on the island — Ibiza Town, Santa Eulalia, San Antonio, Playa d'en Bossa, Es Canar, the northern villages and the Formentera ferry, plus any hotel, villa or address you need.

Do you operate late at night and early morning?

Yes, 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Late-night and early-morning arrivals are fully covered with no unsocial-hours surcharge.

What vehicle will I travel in?

A new Mercedes V-Class — air-conditioned and spacious for up to seven passengers with generous luggage room, complimentary water and WiFi on board.

How do I book an airport transfer?

Book via WhatsApp or email. We confirm within 30 minutes and send full driver details — name, phone and vehicle plate — 24 hours before your transfer. Payment by card, cash or bank transfer.
