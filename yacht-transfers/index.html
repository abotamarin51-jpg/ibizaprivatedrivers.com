Use the real pedestrian meeting point

Marinas and ports can have controlled vehicle access, several gates and different pedestrian pickup areas. Send the exact marina or port, yacht or berth reference when available, and the practical meeting point confirmed for passengers.

For airport arrivals, include the flight number and luggage. For yacht disembarkations, share the expected ready time rather than only the vessel arrival time.

Ibiza Private Drivers provides ground transport. Yacht charter, ferry tickets, marina access permissions and vessel services are separate unless explicitly arranged through the relevant provider.
