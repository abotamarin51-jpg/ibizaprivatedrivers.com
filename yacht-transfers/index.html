FAQ

Yacht & port transfer questions

Do you provide transfers to and from yachts in Ibiza?

Private transfers can be requested for owners, guests and crew at confirmed Ibiza marinas and ports. Send the marina, yacht or berth details and a reachable contact.

How much does a yacht or port transfer cost?

Pricing depends on the marina, practical meeting point, destination, schedule, passengers, luggage and vehicle requirements.

Which marinas and ports do you cover?

Journeys can be requested for principal ports and marinas including Marina Ibiza, Marina Botafoch, Ibiza Port, Santa Eulalia and San Antonio, subject to access and availability.

Can you transfer crew as well as owners and guests?

Yes. We regularly handle crew transfers, provisioning runs and guest movements for yachts in Ibiza, and coordinate timing around tenders, berthing and departures.

What vehicle is used?

A Mercedes-Benz V-Class can be requested, with passenger, luggage and vehicle requirements confirmed before booking.

Are you available for late or early sailings?

Early and late bookings can be requested. Availability and any response to schedule changes must be confirmed for the specific booking.
