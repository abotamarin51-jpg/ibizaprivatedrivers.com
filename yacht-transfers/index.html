The service

Marina pickups planned around real access conditions

A yacht or marina transfer works best when the practical vehicle meeting point is confirmed before travel. Send the marina or port, yacht or berth information when useful, pickup time, destination, passenger count, luggage and a reachable contact.

Vehicle access can differ between marinas, berths and event periods, so the pickup point is confirmed operationally rather than assuming the car can always reach the quay.

For broader island itineraries beyond a marina transfer, use our Private Driver Ibiza hub or Chauffeur Service Ibiza.

FAQ

Yacht & marina transfer questions

Do you provide transfers to and from yachts in Ibiza?

Yes. Private transfers can be requested for owners, guests and crew at confirmed Ibiza marinas and ports. Send the marina, practical meeting point, schedule, passengers, luggage and a reachable contact.

How much does a yacht or marina transfer cost?

Pricing depends on the confirmed marina or port, meeting point, destination, schedule, passengers, luggage, stops and vehicle requirements.

Which Ibiza marinas and ports can I request?

Journeys can be requested for principal ports and marinas including Marina Ibiza, Marina Botafoch, Ibiza Port, Santa Eulalia and San Antonio, subject to practical access and confirmed availability.

Can you connect Ibiza Airport with a marina or yacht?

Yes. Send the flight number, scheduled arrival, marina or port, passenger count, luggage and practical meeting details so the transfer plan can be confirmed.

Can hotel guests transfer directly to a marina?

Yes. Pre-booked journeys can connect hotels or resorts with confirmed marinas and ports, subject to driver and vehicle availability.
