FAQ

Yacht & port transfer questions

Do you provide transfers to and from yachts in Ibiza?

Yes. We provide private transfers for yacht owners, guests and crew arriving or departing by sea, covering Marina Ibiza, Marina Botafoch, Santa Eulalia and every port on the island.

How much does a yacht or port transfer cost?

Pricing depends on the marina and your destination. All rates are fixed and all-inclusive with no meter. Tell us the port and the drop-off and we will confirm an exact price.

Which marinas and ports do you cover?

All of them — Marina Ibiza, Marina Botafoch, the Ibiza commercial port, Santa Eulalia marina, San Antonio and smaller harbours, including connections to and from the airport.

Can you transfer crew as well as owners and guests?

Yes. We regularly handle crew transfers, provisioning runs and guest movements for yachts in Ibiza, and coordinate timing around tenders, berthing and departures.

What vehicle is used?

A new Mercedes V-Class — air-conditioned and spacious for up to seven passengers with generous luggage room, complimentary water and WiFi on board.

Are you available for late or early sailings?

Yes, 24 hours a day, every day. Early departures, late arrivals and last-minute changes around sailing times are fully covered with no unsocial-hours surcharge.
