Before you fly

Pre-book the journeys that matter most

Travellers arriving from the United States or United Kingdom often have fixed flight, hotel, villa, restaurant and nightlife plans. Pre-booking is most useful when a missed connection, unsuitable vehicle or last-minute availability would disrupt the wider itinerary.

For one confirmed pickup and destination, use a private transfer. For several stops, waiting time or a vehicle needed for an agreed period, use Chauffeur Ibiza. For broad island-wide private-driver planning, start with Private Driver Ibiza.

The United States and United Kingdom are customer-origin markets for this guide. The road service itself is provided in Ibiza.

Airport arrival

What to send before landing at Ibiza Airport

Flight details

Send the airline or flight number and scheduled arrival time so the booking can be coordinated using confirmed flight information.

Passengers & luggage

State the real passenger count and luggage. Large suitcases or several travellers can affect the suitable vehicle arrangement.

Exact destination

Provide the hotel, villa map pin, marina or exact destination rather than only a general Ibiza area.

Separate arrivals

If guests arrive on different flights, explain whether separate pickups are required or whether the group plans to depart together.

United States

Planning Ibiza transport from the USA

For long-haul journeys, send the final flight arriving into Ibiza rather than only the original departure from the United States. If the itinerary connects through Madrid, Barcelona, London or another European airport, the Ibiza flight number is the most useful reference for the local pickup plan.

Confirm all service times in local Ibiza time. For larger luggage loads after a long-haul trip, provide the actual number and size of suitcases before confirmation rather than relying only on passenger capacity.

For a full stay involving a resort, yacht, restaurants and nightlife, a Multi-Day Chauffeur Ibiza request can be clearer than booking many isolated journeys.

United Kingdom

Planning Ibiza transport from the UK

For UK departures, confirm the Ibiza arrival flight and the local Ibiza pickup time. During the main summer season, Ibiza is one hour ahead of the United Kingdom, so service times should be written in local Ibiza time to avoid ambiguity.

If the trip combines airport, hotel, beach club, dinner and nightlife, send the entire plan rather than booking only the first journey. This allows the correct distinction between private transfers and an hourly or full-day chauffeur.

FAQ

USA & UK traveller questions

Should US and UK travellers pre-book a private driver in Ibiza?

Pre-booking is useful when the pickup time, destination, passengers, luggage or wider itinerary matters. Availability and booking terms should be confirmed before travel.

Is a chauffeur better than individual transfers for several stops?

A chauffeur booking is generally more suitable when several stops, waiting time or a vehicle for an agreed period are required. A transfer is designed for a confirmed point-to-point journey.

What information should I send before arriving?

Send the date, pickup time, exact pickup and destination, passengers, luggage, return, flight number for airport arrivals and expected duration for chauffeur bookings.

Can private aviation passengers arrange ground transport?

Yes. Send the confirmed flight or handling reference, passengers, luggage and authorised landside meeting point. Restricted or airside access is not assumed.
