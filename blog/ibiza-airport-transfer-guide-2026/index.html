Before you book

Send the details that actually determine the transfer

A useful Ibiza Airport transfer request should include the date, flight number, scheduled arrival or departure, passenger count, luggage and exact destination. Those details matter more than a generic request for “airport transport” because the vehicle and pickup plan depend on the real group and route.

If you need a child seat, larger luggage capacity, several vehicles or a specific vehicle arrangement, include that before confirmation. For arrivals, the practical meeting point and contact method should be agreed as part of the booking rather than assumed.

For the main airport service, see Ibiza Airport Transfers. For several stops or a vehicle retained for longer, use the Private Chauffeur Ibiza page instead.

Hotels & resorts

Airport to hotel transfers in Ibiza

For a hotel transfer, send the exact hotel name and passenger details. If the property has several entrances, a private road or a restricted drop-off zone, the practical vehicle point should be confirmed before travel.

Luxury hotel guests can use the dedicated Luxury Hotel Transfers Ibiza hub, with specific pages for Nobu Hotel Ibiza Bay, Six Senses Ibiza, 7Pines Resort Ibiza, Ibiza Gran Hotel, Destino Five Ibiza and The Standard, Ibiza.

Private villas

Airport to villa: the map pin matters

Ibiza villas may be reached by rural roads, gated entrances or private access tracks. Send the exact map pin, property or gate name, access instructions and a reachable villa contact. A villa name alone is not always enough to identify the correct entrance.

Passenger and luggage details are especially important for villa arrivals because several large suitcases or separate flight groups may change the required vehicle plan. See Villa Transfers Ibiza for the complete booking checklist.

Marinas & yachts

Connect Ibiza Airport with a marina or yacht

For marina transport, include the marina or port, practical meeting point, passengers and luggage. Yacht or berth information can help when it identifies the correct pedestrian collection area, but vehicle access to a quay should never be assumed.

Use Yacht & Marina Transfers Ibiza for airport, hotel and villa connections to Marina Botafoch, Marina Ibiza, Ibiza Port, Santa Eulalia and San Antonio.

Nightlife arrivals

Airport, hotel check-in and nightlife on the same day

If the plan continues from the airport to a hotel or villa and then to nightlife later, list every requested journey. A one-way airport transfer does not automatically reserve an evening return or a driver for several hours.

For evening transport, see Ibiza Night Driver and Ibiza Club Transfers. Venue access and practical pickup points can change during busy periods, so the confirmed meeting instruction is more useful than assuming door access.

Formentera connection

What “Ibiza Airport to Formentera” means

The road journey is from Ibiza Airport to the confirmed ferry terminal in Ibiza Port. Ferry tickets, ferry schedules and onward road transport in Formentera are separate unless they are explicitly confirmed as additional services.

This distinction matters when comparing quotes: an airport-to-port road transfer is not the same product as a complete onward journey after the ferry.

FAQ

Ibiza Airport transfer questions

What details should I send for an Ibiza Airport transfer?

Date, flight number, scheduled arrival or departure time, passenger count, luggage, exact destination and any child-seat or vehicle requirements.

Can I pre-book Ibiza Airport transport to a villa?

Yes. Include the exact villa map pin, access instructions, a property contact, passengers and luggage so the transfer can be planned before confirmation.

Can I book Ibiza Airport to a marina or yacht?

Yes. Provide the marina or port, practical meeting point, passengers, luggage and yacht or berth information when useful.

Does an Ibiza Airport to Formentera booking include the ferry?

The road transfer can be planned from Ibiza Airport to Ibiza Port. Ferry tickets and onward transport in Formentera are separate unless explicitly confirmed.
