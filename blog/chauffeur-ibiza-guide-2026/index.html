Chauffeur or transfer?

Choose the service that matches the real itinerary

A direct transfer is usually best for one confirmed pickup and destination. A chauffeur booking makes more sense when the day includes several movements, waiting time, a flexible schedule or a vehicle that needs to remain connected to the itinerary for an agreed period.

For the main commercial chauffeur service, use Chauffeur Service Ibiza. If the requirement is broader and you are still deciding between transfer and chauffeur formats, start with Private Driver Ibiza.

What to send

A chauffeur quote needs more than a pickup address

Send the date, start time, exact pickup, estimated duration, passenger count and all known stops. Add luggage, child-seat requirements, villa access information, marina meeting points and the final return where relevant.

If the schedule is flexible, explain which parts are fixed and which may change. Changes to duration, route, passenger group or the number of stops can affect availability and pricing, so the revised arrangement should be confirmed.

Daytime

Restaurants, beaches, marinas and several stops

A chauffeur itinerary can connect a hotel or villa with several planned destinations during the day. The advantage is not unlimited travel; it is having one confirmed operational plan for the requested duration and stops.

For villas, exact map pins and gate access matter. For marinas, practical pedestrian meeting points matter. For restaurants and busy areas, the closest legal and practical vehicle point may be more useful than assuming door access.

Evening

Private chauffeur for nightlife and late returns

Evening chauffeur bookings can cover restaurants, clubs, private events and the final return when those movements are included in the confirmed itinerary. Venue access may change during busy periods, so the practical pickup instruction should be followed.

For a nightlife-specific transfer rather than a retained chauffeur booking, see Ibiza Club Transfers or Ibiza Night Driver.

Chauffeur Ibiza questions

Can I hire a chauffeur by the hour in Ibiza?

Hourly chauffeur bookings can be requested, subject to confirmed driver and vehicle availability. Send the requested start time, duration, pickup, passengers and itinerary.

What is the difference between a chauffeur and a transfer?

A transfer is typically one confirmed pickup and destination. A chauffeur booking is more suitable for several stops, waiting time or a vehicle retained for an agreed period.

Can I book a full-day chauffeur in Ibiza?

Yes, subject to confirmed availability. Send the start time, expected duration, known stops, passenger count and final return.

Can I use a chauffeur for nightlife?

Evening and late-night chauffeur itineraries can be requested by reservation. Include venues, expected duration, passenger details and the return plan.
