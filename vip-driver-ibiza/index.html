Operational planning

Discretion starts with a clear itinerary

A VIP driver booking is not defined by a label or a guest’s public profile. It is defined by accurate operational details: who travels, where the vehicle can stop, which contact coordinates the group and what must happen if the schedule changes.

Send the complete itinerary before confirmation. Include every airport arrival, hotel, villa, restaurant, marina, event or return journey, together with the requested time and passenger group. A direct transfer and a vehicle kept available for several hours are different services and require different planning.

Nominate one lead contact—such as the principal guest, assistant, concierge, security coordinator or villa manager—who can confirm changes. Too many independent instructions create avoidable confusion. The booking contact should keep passenger and access information current and remain reachable around each pickup.

For Ibiza Airport arrivals, provide every flight number, landing time, passenger count and luggage requirement. If guests arrive separately, explain whether each passenger needs an individual collection or whether the group will wait for one coordinated departure.

For villas, marinas and events, share the exact map pin or operational meeting point. Vehicle access may be restricted, temporary or unsuitable at some entrances. A practical pedestrian meeting point is agreed rather than promising access that cannot be guaranteed.

Service boundaries

Transport confirmed separately from other services

This page describes private-driver transport. Event tickets, table or restaurant reservations, accreditation, security, close protection, yacht arrangements and property access are not included unless they are explicitly listed in a written confirmation.

Privacy is treated as an operational priority. Passenger information should be limited to what is needed to complete the booking, and special requests should be sent through the nominated contact rather than published in open messages.

Several vehicles can be requested for larger groups, assistants, luggage or separate schedules. Availability, vehicle type and coordination must be confirmed for the specific date. Last-minute additions cannot be assumed.

Send schedule changes as early as possible. A new time, pickup point or destination may change the required vehicle plan, and the revised arrangement is only valid once confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

Before confirming a VIP driver

What details are needed for a VIP driver booking?

Send the date, full schedule, exact pickup points, passenger count, luggage, lead contact, requested vehicle arrangement and every return or intermediate stop.

Can an assistant or concierge coordinate the booking?

Yes. Nominate one lead contact and provide the itinerary, passenger names when appropriate, property access and any operational notes before confirmation.

Are event tickets, reservations or security included?

No. This page describes private transport. Venue access, tickets, reservations, security and other concierge services are separate unless explicitly included in a written confirmation.

Can several vehicles be coordinated?

Several vehicles can be requested. Send every passenger group, arrival, pickup and destination so availability and the coordination plan can be reviewed.
