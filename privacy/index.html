What Data We Collect

We collect the information you voluntarily provide when requesting a quote or booking by WhatsApp, telephone or email, such as name, contact details, pickup and drop-off information, travel dates, passenger details and other information needed to prepare the requested service.

Purpose

Your data is used to prepare quotes, manage requested transport services and communicate with you about the booking. We do not sell personal data to advertisers.

Legal Basis

The legal basis for processing is the performance of pre-contractual measures or the service you request and, where applicable, your consent when contacting us.

Analytics and Cookies

With your consent, this website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and interactions such as clicks on WhatsApp, telephone and email links. Analytics may process technical information including the page visited, approximate country, device type and interaction time. Advertising storage and advertising personalisation remain disabled.

Analytics storage is denied by default and is enabled only after you select “Accept Analytics” in the consent notice. You can withdraw or change your choice at any time by selecting change analytics preference. For information about Google's processing, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary for the requested service and any applicable legal or administrative obligations.

Your Rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, objection or restriction of processing where applicable by emailing info@ibizaprivatedrivers.com. You may also contact the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) regarding your data-protection rights.
